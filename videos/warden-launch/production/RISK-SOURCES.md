# Evidence behind the launch film's risk examples

Researched 2026-09-07. These sources support the risk categories and everyday actions in the opening. They do not validate Warden's effectiveness. All names, records, prompts, amounts, interfaces, and employees shown in the film are fictional illustrations. Do not attach company logos, news screenshots, or statistics to these representative sequences.

## 1. Client information used in ordinary sales/support prompts

[Harmonic Security — GenAI in the Enterprise: It’s Getting Personal](https://www.harmonic.security/resources/genai-in-the-enterprise-its-getting-personal), published November 18, 2025.

**Evidence:** This primary vendor telemetry report describes customer-data exposures originating from sales and support staff who used AI to draft or translate material containing client information. Its customer categories include profiles, billing details, authentication data, and transactions. It also reports internal code submitted for debugging or optimization.

**Scope and limits:** Anonymized browser activity from participating US/UK organizations during July–September 2025; native mobile and direct API use are excluded. These are classified exposure events in a vendor's customer sample, not a representative rate for every company. The public report does not identify individual clients, prove a subsequent public leak, or establish which events reached providers despite controls. Do not claim that the data became public, trained a model, or caused financial loss.

**Film use:** A fictional employee includes client contact details while drafting a message. “Client data. Exposed.” means exposure to an external AI service in this illustrated request, not publication to the internet. Show the actual submit action and destination.

## 2. Proprietary code and confidential business documents sent to AI

[Netskope Threat Labs — Cloud and Threat Report: Generative AI 2025](https://www.netskope.com/resources/reports-guides/cloud-and-threat-report-generative-ai-2025).

**Evidence:** Netskope documents detections involving source code, regulated data, intellectual property, and credentials sent to generative AI apps against organizational policies. It specifically describes customer lists and contracts being used for analysis, and sensitive source code being sent for editing or summarization.

**Scope and limits:** Authorized anonymized activity from a subset of Netskope customers, February 1, 2024–February 28, 2025. Its methodology explicitly says the detections do not account for each event's impact. These categories establish observed risky sharing, not that every transmission became a breach or that every code fragment was proprietary. Keep the movie free of aggregate percentages or claims about all employees.

**Film use:** A fictional internal pricing or roadmap document enters an AI request. “Company secrets. Shared.” is a representative confidentiality risk. A manager's configured rule then controls a separate request. No unrelated agent code execution, database deletion, hacking, or damage claims.

## 3. Named historical corroboration: Samsung's internal source code

[Bloomberg reporting, published by Bloomberg Línea — Samsung proíbe funcionários de usar o ChatGPT após vazamento de código-fonte](https://www.bloomberglinea.com.br/2023/05/02/samsung-proibe-funcionarios-de-usar-o-chatgpt-apos-vazamento-de-codigo-fonte/), May 2, 2023.

**Evidence:** Bloomberg reported from an internal Samsung memorandum that engineers uploaded internal source code to ChatGPT in April 2023. The report states that a Samsung representative confirmed the memo restricting generative AI use.

**Scope and limits:** This is direct newsroom reporting of company documents and a spokesperson's confirmation, not a public Samsung incident report. The article says the exact contents were unclear. It does not establish public dissemination, downstream model training, competitor access, or financial harm. It describes a historical restriction, not Samsung's current AI policy. This source corroborates the code-upload risk only; it does not support meeting-note details.

**Film use:** Background evidence only. Do not portray Samsung as a Warden user or suggest Warden stopped this incident.

Approve it. Warden checks connected requests before they reach the model. Block private data. Hold sensitive requests for your decision. Let public information through. Connect Claude Code, Codex CLI, or compatible tools. Your team keeps moving. You stay in control. Download Warden.

The rule-creation and enforcement phrases are product claims to match against the actual Warden implementation and shown workflow. The risk reports do not establish those capabilities. Keep “connected requests” and qualified tool compatibility; do not turn them into a universal protection claim.
