## Story

A familiar dark Workspace sends a confidential client brief to an external AI tool. Contact, bank and pricing fields visibly cross the company boundary. These are fictional examples of information leaving a company, not evidence of public disclosure or model training.

The manager gives a business goal: **Help our team write client emails without exposing confidential information.** The prompt submits quickly, docks at the top and visibly leads into proposed rules. Complete rule rows arrive in a short cascade. The administrator reviews them and separately activates them. Example protections are Client contact details — BLOCK, Bank account details — BLOCK, and Non-public pricing — REVIEW. No fixed rule count or generation-time promise appears.

Show connected private-data blocking, human review of a sensitive request and allowed public work. Product, Engineering and Operations use their configured **Claude Code**, **Codex** or compatible tools. The visible label and narration both say Codex, without CLI.

Finish with the approved v8 extruded official shield and letters-only wordmark, one mint **Download for free** action, **Open source. Free for everyone.**, platform labels and the repository address. The entire outro holds continuously and retains its exact internal choreography.

Enforcement applies to connected requests and configured policies; the email-writing example does not imply interception of every Gmail, Outlook or unconnected tool. Proposed drafts remain inactive until the administrator activates them.
