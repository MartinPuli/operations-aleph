# Warden website visual direction

Mode: Persuade. The user explicitly chose the existing centered landing and its narrative over a new layout. Preserve that page; improve its effects, motion, color and product presentation with the approved launch-film identity.

## Existing story
Centered statement: “Writing the rules down is not the same as enforcing them.” A typed request hits the luminous gate. The same day continues through four original scenes: describe/review/activate policy, request/refusal/connected tools, the decision record, and a held usage request. Native scrolling, no pinned scroll or hijacking. Each scene plays once after its product panel is sufficiently visible, with manual steps and replay for reading at any pace.

## Refinements
Ink #081119, warm ivory #f0ede4, mint #a2edce, coral #f17e8b, amber #ebc478. Inter Variable. Preserve the centered hero and meaningful gate light. Mint means brand/protection/activation; coral is a block; amber is human review. The user requested more presence in the formerly empty hero-to-story handoff, a crafted policy editor and a stronger 3D close. The handoff now pairs the existing three-line statement with an actual shield, incoming request and decision record. On wide screens, the first chapter puts narration beside the light product surface; full policy text belongs in expandable rule details, with human-readable titles leading the rows.

## Motion
One incoming signal and a coral verdict wave in the original WebGL light; bounded scene entrances and causal product transitions. Avoid generic particles, constant motion, scroll traps and letter-by-letter headline animation. Manual controls can stop autoplay. Reduced motion presents the complete story. Both sculptural checkpoint and closing object use the film’s official silver/mint 3D geometry. They enter in 1.9 seconds, respond subtly to a fine pointer, settle, and pause offscreen. The footer places the object next to the closing message and download actions on desktop so they are seen together.

## Finish
Keep the original closing sentence and downloads. The approved 26-second film opens on request. No claims of universal tool interception, zero data leaving the machine, tamper-proof records or guaranteed hard spend caps.
